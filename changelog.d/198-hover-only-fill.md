- **Hover-only fill for text-like controls:** links and breadcrumbs, nav and menu items, options, list, tree and table rows, tabs, ghost and toolbar icon buttons, disclosure triggers, and close, copy and remove buttons now rest transparent and fill on hover (`--poodle-style-control-fillHover`) and on press (`--poodle-style-control-fillActive`), instead of showing the resting fill permanently. Value-holding controls keep their resting fill. Static text such as number-input affixes and the selection-summary overflow count has no fill.
