**Toolchain and React peers.** Contributors and CI use Bun 1.4.5 from `packageManager`; Bun 1.4.0 to 1.4.2 refuse nested `file:` dependencies. Declaration packaging uses `@sveltejs/package` 3, which needs Node 22 or later; the emitted Svelte declarations are unchanged. For consumers, `@inflatable-cookie/poodle-react` now caps its `react` and `react-dom` peers at `>=18 <20` (was `>=18`), the majors it is tested on.
