## Why It Matters

shared ToggleGroup transition and report the complete next open selection.
Rust has two competing mode fields, cannot represent a collapsed single result,
and reports only the clicked item so the GPUI specimen rebuilds membership
itself. Its instance-id builder is also a no-op, and the mounted tree lacks the
contracted trigger/panel semantics. The operator approved the clean pre-1.0
break needed to remove those seams.
