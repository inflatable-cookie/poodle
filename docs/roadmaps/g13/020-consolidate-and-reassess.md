# g13.020 Consolidate And Reassess

Status: planned
Owner: Poodle maintainer
Depends on: `g13.019`

## Objective

Use the single vocabulary authority as the vantage point for the reassessment
the `g13.008` verdict was recorded to enable.

Once every component's vocabulary lives in one place, the corpus can be judged
from one authority instead of four hand-maintained copies. That is the whole
reason `revise` was chosen over `reject`.

## Deliverables

- Consolidation candidates the single authority makes visible: duplicated
  vocabulary, near-identical components, value domains that disagree across
  runtimes, parts that exist in one runtime and not others.
- A re-measurement against the `g13.002` frozen baseline and the `g13.008`
  ledger: corpus LOC, duplication removed, drift caught.
- A judgement on whether the vocabulary authority earned its keep, with the
  same honesty the pilot verdict used — including the option of retiring it.

## Acceptance

- The reassessment names what the authority did **not** deliver, not only what
  it did.
- Any further programme is compiled from measured evidence, not from the
  momentum of this one.
