# g14.008 — Pilot Verdict

Status: complete — **reject**
Decided: 2026-08-15
Depends on: `g14.001`–`g14.007`, `g14.023`

## Verdict

Reject the executable component-conformance mechanism as Poodle's standing
cross-runtime architecture. Do not open `g14.009`–`g14.014`.

The pilot caught valuable component and backend defects. Its headless GPUI
execution repair is also worth retaining. Those results do not make the
mechanism adoptable. It failed four of the five explicit admission rules,
including its cost stop condition, and the apparent six-profile green gate
omitted HistoryCenter from the comparator.

`g14.021` is the only ready next task. It removes the rejected authority,
preserves product fixes and focused regressions, and leaves Poodle with honest
gates before another parity architecture is designed.

## Rule Result

| Admission rule | Result | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One pipeline covers all six profiles in Svelte, React, and GPUI | **fail** | The comparator registered only Button, RangeSlider, Tabs, Popover, and TextInput. Registering HistoryCenter and its `level`, `scrollable`, and `maxHeight` fields produces **1,205** cross-runtime problems. |
| Required active-runtime absence stays red | **fail** | HistoryCenter execution reports existed, but omission from the manual comparator registry let the completion board pass without comparing them. |
| Generic tooling stays component-neutral | **fail** | The serializer, comparator, Effigy typecheck command, GPUI headless board, and GPUI fixture support all maintain component lists or component-specific branches. That duplication caused the silent omission. |
| Rust boundary stays backend-neutral | **fail** | Crate dependencies remain clean, but `poodle-render::primitive_probes` contains `probe_focus_gpui`, `probe_activate_gpui`, and `gpui.*` evidence fields. GPUI semantics leaked into shared evidence code. |
| Ongoing cost is lower than duplication removed | **fail** | The corrected report measures **22,746 source LOC** and **259,185 generated bytes** against **472 LOC** of replaced hand-written source. The mechanism is roughly 48 times the source it replaced. |

The first and fifth failures independently require reassessment under spec
066. Correcting all four failed dimensions would mean replacing authority,
registration, comparison semantics, and the cost model together. That is a
new architecture, not a bounded `revise` branch.

## Authority And Coverage Audit

- Button, Tabs, Popover, and TextInput Svelte/React shells consume their
  inferred portable props/events.
- RangeSlider and HistoryCenter shells retain independent public interface
  declarations. The portable interface modules type their case corpora, not
  both shipped web implementations.
- Generated Rust declarations exist for Button, Tabs, Popover, TextInput, and
  HistoryCenter. RangeSlider retains its hand-written structured spec.
- Component registration is repeated across serializer, comparator, task
  command, GPUI board, and native fixture support. There is no derived roster
  that can prove a corpus participates in every required stage.
- The primitive report has 21 passing owned rows, but still marks
  `semantic.selected`, modified activation, key, scrub, drag/drop, context,
  and all three input rows as deferred. That contradicts the profile logs
  claiming those rows joined the gate.
- GPUI mounted accessibility remains forced acceptance under contract 003,
  not observed backend parity.

## Corrected Comparator Result

The g14.008 audit adds HistoryCenter to the comparator and restores the three
fields introduced by g14.007. `effigy conformance:complete` then passes the
per-runtime web and GPUI suites but fails comparison with **1,205 problems**.
Repeated classes include:

- web/GPUI parent-tree disagreement around root, surface, list, rows, entries,
  and disclosures
- accessible names and text present on web but absent in GPUI
- focus and focus-visible disagreement between Svelte, React, and GPUI
- `controls`, `expanded`, `focusedText`, and event-payload shape differences
- picker role disagreement (`button` versus `combobox`)
- host-action labels and status/empty/rejection text differences

These are not one normalization typo. They cross structure, accessibility,
focus, role, state, text, and event semantics. Making the gate green requires
a fresh statement of what portable parity means for composites.

## Value Retained

The rejected mechanism found real defects that existing checks missed:

- Button double activation and missing GPUI focus/accessibility/token evidence
- RangeSlider dual-thumb, scrub, controlled-value, and backend interaction
  defects
- Tabs repeated identity, focus movement, and runtime selection defects
- Popover nested dismissal order, disabled controlled-open, placement, and
  focus-restoration defects
- TextInput non-BMP selection, editing order, IME, search-clear, and native
  input-buffer defects
- HistoryCenter hierarchy, command payload, rename, focus, scroll-bound, and
  disclosure defects inside each runtime

Retain the component fixes, renderer/node improvements, headless GPUI test
platform, and focused regression coverage. Do not retain the universal
interface/case/observation plane merely to preserve those results.

The specimen experiment supplied negative evidence too: exhaustive corpus
projection replaced teaching examples with Cartesian matrices. The curated
Button, RangeSlider, and Tabs pages were restored. `g14.026` continues the
human-centred catalogue work without a Conformance tab.

## Runtime And Jetstream Assessment

`g14.023` fixed the unacceptable foreground runner: the GPUI test body now
runs headlessly in about 0.05 seconds and no longer steals focus for 15+
minutes. Keep that infrastructure.

Headlessness did not make completion dependable. A fast board that silently
skips a corpus is worse than an explicit partial board. The standing selector
is intentionally red after the comparator correction until `g14.021` removes
or replaces the rejected claim.

Jetstream admission is not a small renderer hookup. The current GPUI-equivalent
driver, primitive probes, headless board, and six component adapters total
roughly 4.2k LOC before any Jetstream renderer capability repair. Cases are
data-portable, but the evidence and interaction machinery is backend work.
Do not use prospective Jetstream reuse as adoption credit.
