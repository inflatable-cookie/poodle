# Fidelity tables

Round five. Generated by `node tools/fidelity.js` (plain tint, all three references and the
preset table) and `node tools/fidelity.js --even solarized-light` from the engine in `index.html`.
ΔE is the OKLab distance × 100; about 2 is the smallest difference visible side by side, above 10
reads as a different colour. ΔL, ΔC and Δh are derived minus canonical, in OKLCH (Δh is n/a when
either colour is near grey).

What moved since round four: only `accent.fill`. Layers, text, status, syntax and `accent.text`
are byte-identical.

- **Light or vivid accents** (Eclipse, Graphite, Hornet, Forest, Nord) were already exact and stay
  exact: their picked colour carries 7:1 text, so it's used as picked.
- **Mid-tone accents move further than in round four**, because the fill now aims for 7:1 text
  instead of 4.5:1 text plus 3:1 against the layers. Accent fill against today's themes: Iceberg
  0.0 → 6.8 (deeper blue, white text), Clay 3.9 → 9.5 and Meadow 2.6 → 11.4 (lighter, dark text
  instead of white), Midnight 0.0 → 4.0, Rose 0.0 → 3.6, Cobalt 0.0 → 0.6, Solarized 0.0 → 4.9.
  Solarized Light against canonical blue: 5.2 → 8.5.
- **The authored colour is still in the theme**: `accent.line` (focus ring, tab underline,
  selected row, edges) is the picked colour in every one of these presets, because it already
  clears 3:1 on their layers.
