# 0.4.10 — 2026-10-06

`0.4.10` is a patch candidate with one fix and no API change. Peer ranges stay
`svelte >=5.56.8 <6`, `react` / `react-dom >=18`, and `marked ^18.0.9`.

## Public-intent changes

- Collapsible and Accordion panels open at full content height in Svelte and
  React, under every motion policy; previously they could stay clipped to 0px
  after opening (present since 2026-09-01). Components that compose them, such
  as ModelCatalogueEditor's hidden-models section, are fixed with them.
- Svelte and React require `@inflatable-cookie/poodle-core >=0.4.10 <0.5`.

Internal preview and tooling packages remain at `0.0.0`.

## Downstream checks

- Remove any workaround that forced Collapsible or Accordion content open or
  overrode `.poodle-collapsible__content-clip` height, and check panels open to
  their content and follow content changes.
- Refresh consumer lockfiles and verify they resolve core `>=0.4.10 <0.5`.
- The release candidate certificate must build, pack and source-free-install
  the core and Svelte archive set.

## Candidate boundary

This note records the `0.4.10` npm/web candidate. Core and Svelte are the only
publication set. React remains private. Cargo packages, native evidence,
crates.io, Jetstream admission and windowed conformance are outside this patch.
