# Poodle Code of Conduct

Poodle should be a practical, respectful place to build shared infrastructure.
This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and project spaces represented as official Poodle channels.

## Expected Conduct

- Discuss the work and its effects without attacking the person doing it.
- Give actionable feedback and assume mistakes can be corrected.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and access
  needs.
- Keep disagreements focused on contracts, evidence, tradeoffs, and outcomes.
- Protect private information and follow the security-reporting boundary.

## Unacceptable Conduct

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or sustained personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or unwanted sexual attention
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Deliberate disruption, intimidation, or bad-faith participation
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to
[tom@inflatablecookie.com](mailto:tom@inflatablecookie.com). Include relevant
links, dates, context, and any immediate safety concern. Reports will be handled
as privately as practical, though investigation may require limited disclosure
to the people responsible for resolving the issue.

Security vulnerabilities follow the separate private process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, issue a
warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. The response
will reflect the severity, impact, history, and willingness to correct the
behavior. Good-faith reports will not be punished.
